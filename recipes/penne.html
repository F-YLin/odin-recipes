Penne

This is a photo of penne

Ingredients

  • (1 lb) box penne or (1 lb) box penne rigate, cooked as directed, drained and kept hot
  • tablespoons butter or 2 tablespoons margarine
  • garlic cloves, minced
  • tablespoons flour
  • 3/4 cup chicken broth or 3/4 cup beef broth
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons parsley flakes
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Cook over medium for 1 minute.
  2. Add flour and cook 1 minute, stirring constantly.
  3. Stir in broth and milk and cook, stirring frequently, until sauce boils and thickens.
  4. Add parsley, salt, pepper and cheese.
  5. Stir until cheese is melted.
  6. Toss hot pasta with sauce and serve immediately.
